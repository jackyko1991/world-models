# History images

Where Wikimedia Commons has a freely licensed picture that fits a card (a portrait of the person, or a photo of the
thing), the card shows it. Each licence was checked on the file page itself (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA
only), and the site shows the author and licence under every picture, linked to its Commons page. No screenshots of
commercial products or games and no generated images are used.

Every other card shows an **original diagram** drawn for this site by `scripts/history_svgs.py` (Apache-2.0, like the
code). The diagrams show each card's idea, never a likeness of a person; the `.svg` files stay in this folder for all
cards as fallbacks.
